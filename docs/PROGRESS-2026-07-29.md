### Notes / decisions worth Tyler's attention

1. **The task brief's challenge setup numbers were swapped.** The official Challenge Survival
   Guide says **Highest Bidder = 10 grey + 1 purple** (no player-count table) and
   **Pull or Steal = 1 purple + grey per table (3p→2, 4p→3, 5p→4, 6p→5)**, i.e. exactly one rock
   per player. The brief had these the other way round. Implemented per the PDF.
2. **Eliminated players and Challenges — the brief and the PDF disagree.** The brief says
   eliminated players still take part. That is the **standalone** rule. The **combined-mode**
   guide is explicit: *"If both of your Survivor Character Cards have been voted out you can't
   take part in Challenges."* Since we're implementing combined mode, the PDF wins — eliminated
   players are excluded from Challenge participation.
3. **"deck of 76 Action Cards".** The combined-mode instructions reference adding the Orange
   cards to "the deck of 76 Action Cards", while the rules sheet in `docs/survivor_rules.md`
   says 67. Newer printings may differ. We build from whatever `survivor_cards.json` declares,
   so this needs no code change — worth checking against a physical copy.
4. **Necklace wearer treated as un-votable AND last-resort for the leader's tie-break pick.**
   The expansion says players "can't vote for you"; the standalone sheet says the wearer is
   "safe". So `cast_vote` rejects votes for the wearer, and the tie-break ladder only reaches
   them in tier 3 alongside Immunity Idol players.

### Bugs found *by* the test repair and fixed

1. **Tribal Council Cards could be dealt into opening hands.** `start_full_game` dealt from the
   already-assembled deck, so a 6-player game reliably lost one of its 5 tribal cards to a hand.
   Official setup order is deal (step 3) *then* assemble (step 5) — `create_deck` is now split
   into `create_action_deck` + `assemble_deck`.
2. **Camp Raid never took the drawn card.** `process_card_draw_effects` compared a *resolved*
   copy of the card against the compact object in the player's hand, so the "still in their hand"
   check never matched. That is the entire point of the card; it appears to have never worked.
3. **Sorry For You 500'd.** `handle_reactive_card_play` and `complete_pending_theft` both deleted
   `game["pending_theft"]` a second time after the engine had already cleared it → `KeyError` on
   every reactive play and every declined reaction.
4. **Cards requiring a target were consumed on an invalid target** and the effect silently did
   nothing. `validate_play` now rejects before the card leaves your hand.
5. **A malformed `games.json` crashed the server at startup** (`null`/list/dict-of-non-dicts got
   past `_load` and blew up `garbage_collect`). Now validated, backed up and reset.
6. **`_save` re-raised on I/O failure**, turning a transient disk error into a failed request and
   potentially an aborted game. Now logs and returns False.
7. **Duplicate player names and colors were silently accepted** — the review listed this
   validation as present ✓, but it existed at no layer. Restored via `validate_new_player`
   (+ color-format validation, since colors are written into inline styles).
8. `immunityPlayed` / `hasVoted` were never cleared when a tribal ended.
9. `draw_card` was possible during a tribal council.
10. `_insert_tribal_cards` mutated its caller's list.
11. **Two suites could report success while failing** — they ran a `TextTestRunner` and never set
    an exit code. `tests/test_rules_engine.py` was a pure print script with *no assertions*; it
    now asserts the 67-card official count, deck composition and effect-registry coverage.
